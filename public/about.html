About Us

Trust, built into every device's history.

Authentiqo is led by women entrepreneurs who set out to fix a simple problem: buyers of used and refurbished electronics have never had a reliable way to check a device's real history, and honest sellers have never had a way to prove theirs.

Transparency is the whole premise of our product. Every repair we show is either verified by a registered shop or clearly marked as self-reported. Every ownership change is either tracked by the platform or clearly marked as disclosed by the seller. We'd rather show an honest "we don't know" than a confident-looking number we can't back up.

What we prioritize

  • Clarity over confidence — we label estimates as estimates, and disclosures as disclosures.
  • No unnecessary data collection — we don't collect buyer names or contact details to make this work.
  • A record that can't be quietly rewritten — once something is logged, it stays logged.
