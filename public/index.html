How it works

From workbench to buyer, in four steps

Repairs get logged

Registered repair shops stamp every service permanently to the device's serial number.

Owners register the device

Repair history is pulled in automatically. No plans to resell? You can register a device just to keep its history on record, and give up ownership later if you ever pass it on.

A QR code is generated

It encodes the repair count and ownership transfers, and links straight to the device's page.

Buyers verify instantly

Scan or search a serial number to see the timeline and an algorithmic Trust Score.
