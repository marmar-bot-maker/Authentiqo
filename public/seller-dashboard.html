Register a device

Already registered by someone else? Enter the same serial number under your own account and ownership transfers to you automatically — no separate "transfer" step needed.

Repair history is pulled in automatically from repair shop records — you can't add or edit it here.

Transfer ownership

Authentiqo doesn't collect buyer contact details, so a transfer isn't a button you click here — it happens the moment the new owner registers this same serial number under their own account.

  1. Give the new owner this serial number:
  2. They create a free seller account at /seller-login.html
  3. They enter that serial number under "Register a device" — ownership transfers to them automatically, and this device drops off your list.

Add previous owners

If this device changed hands before it was ever registered on Authentiqo — say, sold privately a couple of times — add that history here. This only adds to the recorded owner count; it can never remove or edit anything already on file, and it's shown to buyers as reported by the seller, not a platform-verified transfer.

Give up ownership

Use this if you bought this device and have no plans to resell it, or if you no longer have it. It disappears from your device list. Nothing about its repair or ownership history is deleted — it's all still visible to buyers exactly as it is now. Anyone with the serial number (you again, or whoever gets it next) can register it in the future.
